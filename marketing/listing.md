# CrazyGames listing draft

**Working title:** Parcel Panic!

**Short description:** Sort parcels onto a busy mailroom shelf. Seal lines, catch sneaky mice, build scoring combos, and collect achievement stamps.

**Description:** Every shift brings 16 deliveries and a new set of missions. Choose one of three parcels, then place it on an open shelf. Four different parcels in a row or column make a seal; a matching 2×2 block makes a tape patch. Mice slip between the shelves and can chew unprotected parcels, so catch them while you can. Score on consecutive turns to build a combo, reroute a tricky hand once per shift, and earn collectible stamps. Complete the missions and beat your personal best.

**How to play:** Tap or click a parcel card, then tap or click an empty shelf. On desktop, press 1, 2, or 3 to choose a card, and R to reroute your hand once. Seal rows or columns with four different parcel types, make 2×2 patches of matching parcels, and place a parcel on a mouse to catch it. Consecutive scoring deliveries earn combo points. A shift ends after 16 deliveries.

**Suggested categories:** Puzzle, Casual, Strategy

**Submission note:** The working title is used by other games. Decide on a distinctive final name before public launch and update the game, covers, videos, and this listing together.
