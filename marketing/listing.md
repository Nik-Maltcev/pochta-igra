# CrazyGames listing draft

**Title:** Mouseproof Mailroom

**Short description:** Sort parcels onto a busy mailroom shelf. Clear the 70-point route by sealing lines, catching mice, and building scoring combos.

**Description:** Aim for 70 points before the shift ends. Choose one of three parcels, then place it on an open shelf. Every delivery scores a point. Four different parcels in a row or column make a seal; a matching 2×2 block makes a tape patch. Mice slip between the shelves and can chew unprotected parcels, so catch them while you can. Earn bonuses on consecutive turns to build a combo, reroute a tricky hand once per shift, complete three missions, and earn collectible stamps. The first shift starts with an easy seal so you can learn by playing.

**How to play:** Tap or click a parcel card, then tap or click an empty shelf. On desktop, press 1, 2, or 3 to choose a card, and R to reroute your hand once. Seal rows or columns with four different parcel types, make 2×2 patches of matching parcels, and place a parcel on a mouse to catch it. Consecutive bonus moves earn combo points. A shift ends at 16 sorted parcels; the first shift has three already in place. Reach 70 points to clear the route. The ? button hides or restores tips.

**Suggested categories:** Puzzle, Casual, Strategy
