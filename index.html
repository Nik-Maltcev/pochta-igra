HOW TO PLAY ＋
  • 📦 Tap a parcel card below, then tap a shelf slot to deliver it.
  • 🩹 Fill a full row or column with 4 different parcels to seal it with tape: +10.
  • ✨ A 2×2 block of one parcel type makes a tape patch: +4.
  • 🐭 Mice sneak in — deliver a parcel onto a mouse to catch it: +5. Hungry mice chew your parcels! Loose mice cost −5 at the end.
  • 📮 Seal a row and a column with one parcel = Double Seal, +10 bonus.
  • 🎯 Complete the 3 goals for extra points. 16 parcels, then the shift is over!
  • 🔥 Score on consecutive deliveries for a growing combo bonus. Reroute your hand once per shift, and collect stamps for special achievements.
